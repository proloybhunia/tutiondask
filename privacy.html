TuitionDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Summary

TuitionDesk is a tool for tuition centres to manage fees, attendance, tests and homework. Your data stays on your own phone. We do not run a server that collects it, and we do not show ads or track you.

What data the app holds

  • Student names, class or batch, and parent mobile numbers
  • Fee amounts, pending fees and payment records
  • Attendance, test scores and homework notes
  • Your centre name and UPI ID

You enter this data yourself. The app does not read your contacts, location, camera or any other phone data.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored in your phone's browser storage on this device only. It is not uploaded to any server and is not shared between phones. Because of this, clearing Chrome's data, uninstalling the app or changing your phone will erase it unless you have saved a backup using the Backup option in the Owner tab.

Sharing

When you tap a button to send a fee reminder or absence message, the app opens WhatsApp with a ready message. The message is shared only when you tap send in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to it. We do not send any data anywhere else.

Children's information

Students may be under 18. The tuition centre owner using the app is responsible for collecting and using student and parent details with the knowledge and consent of parents or guardians, and for keeping the phone secure.

Your control

  • You can edit or remove any student or record inside the app.
  • You can delete everything by uninstalling the app and clearing the site data in Chrome.
  • Keep your backup text private, because it contains all student and parent details.

Security

Your data is protected only by your phone's own security. Use a screen lock and do not share your phone unlocked with others.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For questions, please contact the tuition centre owner who gave you this app.
